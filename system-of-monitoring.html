Project Monitoring Framework

The scheme maintains an active, milestone-driven monitoring process to ensure research accountability and grant effectiveness:

Half-Yearly Reporting

Project progress is assessed half-yearly following DST-SERB / DBT / CSIR templates. Reports are submitted through the online portal. IISc Bangalore serves as the single point of contact for progress data to MoE.

Midterm Evaluations

Online reports are evaluated by the Domain Expert Committee. For midterm reviews, PIs are invited for oral presentations before the expert committee to defend milestone progress and preliminary outputs.

Hand-Holding & Remediation

If project progress faces unexpected experimental hurdles, the committee may recommend close monitoring and assign mentors. In case of unsatisfactory progress or severe unapproved deviations, termination may be recommended.

Apex Oversight

A comprehensive status report of all ongoing projects is prepared by IISc and tabled at every meeting of the Apex Committee chaired by the Secretary (Higher Education).
