Key Objectives

The primary mission of the Scheme for Transformational and Advanced Research in Sciences (STARS) is to fund science projects that are translational, i.e. which have direct implications for the progress of the country, through a competitive process in an open and transparent manner.

1. Funding Translational Science

To fund scientific projects with tangible societal benefits, promoting the translation of basic scientific laboratory breakthroughs into commercial, clinical, or field applications.

2. Problem-First Approach

The basic thrust of the scheme is to take stock of an existing problem and work backwards towards conducting research for a solution.

3. Promoting Interdisciplinary Synergy

Promote an inter-disciplinary and translational approach in research for synergy, de-duplication, and greater comprehensiveness & relevance of research activity across Indian universities and institutes.

4. Addressing National Priorities

Orient science towards addressing critical needs and issues of the country in key sectors like health, agriculture, energy, environment, and national security.

5. International Benchmarks

Attain demonstrable international benchmarks in scientific research quality and publication citations, while securing domestic intellectual property rights for India.
