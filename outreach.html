Online Workshop on Grant Writing

STARS outreach programme for researchers in eligible institutions

Online Workshop on Grant Writing

(December 7–12, 2020)

As part of the STARS outreach programme and to promote effective grant writing, interested faculty from eligible institutions were invited to grant-writing workshops in each scientific domain of the scheme (Physical Sciences, Chemical Sciences, Biological Sciences, Nanosciences, Data Science & Mathematics, and Earth Sciences). A one-day online workshop was conducted for each domain between December 7–12, 2020. Registrations were open from November 1–15, 2020, and the online links to the program were conveyed to participants by November 30, 2020.

Registration is closed.

Program Schedule (PDF)

Online program links were emailed to registered participants. For any queries regarding STARS outreach events, contact support.stars@iisc.ac.in.
