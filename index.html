About the Programme

Scheme for Transformational and Advanced Research in Sciences (STARS) was formulated to fund science projects that have direct implications for the progress of the nation, through a competitive process in an open and transparent manner.

The basic thrust of the scheme is to take stock of an existing problem and work backwards towards conducting research for a solution, promoting an interdisciplinary & translational approach for greater synergy and relevance.

Read Full Details →

Key Objectives

  • Fund Translational Science: Bridge scientific discovery with practical applications in key national sectors.
  • De-duplication: Synergy across premier institutions to maximize research infrastructure usage.
  • Priority Sectors: Health, clean energy, food security, agriculture, defense, and environment.
  • Grant Caps: Up to ₹50 Lakhs for single-PI projects and ₹100 Lakhs for multi-PI collaborative projects.

Six Basic Thrust Areas

The scheme focuses research grants across six designated scientific domains addressing societal challenges with high translational value.

Physical Sciences

Quantum technologies (computing, communication, sensing), photonics, self-assembled soft robotics, and novel superconductors.

View Domain →

Chemical Sciences

Energy conversion and storage, catalysis on demand, small molecule capture ($CO_2$, $H_2$ storage), and functional supramolecular materials.

View Domain →

Biological Sciences

Indian gut microbiome, nutrition, bio-wealth documentation, infectious disease vectors, AMR, and human genomic diversity.

View Domain →

Nanosciences

The SHAPE framework (Security, Healthcare, Agriculture, Pedagogy, Energy & Environment) with verifiable Technology Readiness Levels (TRL).

View Domain →

Data Science & Mathematics

Differential modeling, applied statistics for medicine/weather, number theory for cryptography, medical imaging, and logistics optimization.

View Domain →

Earth Sciences

Deep groundwater reserves, Himalayan glacier budgets, natural hazard risk assessment (earthquakes, tsunamis, landslides), and Critical Zone evolution.

View Domain →
