Important Dates & Milestones

Chronological history of funding cycles, grant submission windows, and national workshops

Chronology of STARS Scheme Milestones

Key dates associated with calls for proposals, evaluation cycles, and capacity-building workshops organized by the National Coordinator Office at IISc Bangalore:

Date Milestone / Event Details & Description
April 22, 2019 Call 1 Launch First official national call for project proposals under MHRD-STARS.
May 31, 2019 Call 1 Deadline Closure of the online submission portal for Call 1 proposals.
October 28, 2019 Call 1 Results Final sanction notifications and approved project lists published.
Dec 7–12, 2020 Grant Writing Workshop — event page National online workshops conducted for faculty across eligible institutions to strengthen proposal formulations.
Dec 28, 2022 Call 2 Launch Launch of STARS Phase 2 with updated priority areas and revamped submission guidelines.
Feb 5, 2023 Call 2 Deadline Final extended submission deadline for Phase 2 proposals.
May 8, 2023 Call 2 Results Announcement of approved research projects across all 6 thrust domains.
Sept 27–29, 2023 Nanobiotechnology Conf. National conference on "Nanobiotechnology: Beyond the Conventional" at IISc Bangalore.
Sept 21, 2024 IP & Patent Workshop Workshop on Patent Filing and Prior Art Search Mechanisms organized for STARS investigators.

About STARS

Scheme for Transformational and Advanced Research in Sciences is funded by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India, and coordinated by the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore.
